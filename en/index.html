02

How I think

Working principles for analytical models, modernization and validation.

Business context.
Verifiable analytical work.

Start with the business question, make the data logic explicit, and review the result against its source.

Define the question before the pipeline

A useful metric needs a clear population, a definition and an agreed interpretation.

Validation is part of the method

A query that executes is not enough: its result must be checked against the expected behavior.

Make responsibilities visible

Separate analysis, implementation, platform dependencies and acceptance.

Keep definitions consistent

Document analytical logic so teams can understand what a metric includes and excludes.

Understand the legacy before changing it

Modernization starts by understanding the business rules and outputs that must be preserved.

The same principles connect the projects and the professional experience.
